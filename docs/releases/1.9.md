# Villager Guard Gunners 1.9

This update expands Quartermaster trading and guard management, improves guard
combat and village navigation, and adds more control over automatic gun loadouts.
It also includes the TaCZ compatibility change from 1.8.1.

## Highlights

- **Quartermaster overview.** Interact with a Quartermaster with both hands empty,
  or select Overview at the station, to see supplies, guard equipment needs,
  service status, and recent deliveries. Visitors can access trades without
  access to private supplies.
- **Bulk guard management.** Apply equipment policies, loadouts, replacement
  permissions, and station assignments to selected guards or loaded squad
  members. Preview the affected guards and supply estimates before applying.
  Larger rosters can be managed in batches of up to 64 guards.
- **Quartermaster trades.** Buy bandages, ammo, ledgers, shields, iron helmets,
  and Recovery Cots as the villager levels up. Optional unloaded gun sales start
  disabled. Datapacks can customize offers across all five levels.
- **Combat decisions.** Guards give greater priority to urgent threats near
  allies, can divide their attention between enemies, and avoid crowding the
  same firing positions. They can make a short local search after losing sight
  of a target. Player orders and rules of engagement retain priority.
- **Navigation.** Guards handle winding paths, short local detours, stairs,
  slabs, and unlit campfire decks more consistently. They can open supported
  wooden doors when their navigation settings allow it and take turns using
  short narrow passages.
- **Village defense and patrols.** Defenders stay near their duty position during
  combat and return afterward. Automatic coverage prefers verified entrances,
  retains reachable parts of large villages, and keeps unaffected patrol trips
  moving during local terrain repairs.
- **Emergency ammo resupply.** Guards with no usable gun ammo can visit a nearby
  Village Armory during combat when there is enough space from threats.
- **Automatic gun loadouts.** New configurations include spawn-egg guards in the
  normal loadout rules. Peaceful difficulty gains separate controls, starting
  with a 35% chance, one nearby automatic gunner, and the Easy pistol pool.

## Fixes

- Unemployed adult villagers can claim Quartermaster stations naturally, and
  replacement villagers can take over stations whose job-site claim was released.
- Quartermaster reports handle incoming deliveries, shared stock visibility,
  unavailable guards, and unloaded roster entries more accurately. Rapid menu
  changes no longer leave the client waiting for a screen that did not open.
- Quartermaster ammo service checks native TaCZ compatibility and skips loose
  ammo requests for guns with dummy or infinite reserves. Quartermaster zombie
  villagers display their profession texture correctly.
- Crowds of nonthreatening mobs no longer prevent nearby threat assessment.
  Target changes preserve patrol limits, and guards stop their old chase before
  searching for a lost enemy.
- Guards recover more reliably from passage waits and interrupted movement.
  Safe local detours are accepted, and positions across walls or on another
  floor are no longer treated as reached.
- Emergency resupply stops when a route becomes hazardous or gun AI is disabled.
  Navigation and village coverage also perform less repeated work.

## Updating from 1.8 or 1.8.1

Replace the mod JAR on both client and server, keep your existing configuration
files, and restart. Existing guard equipment and service settings are retained.
Existing building datapacks can stay installed.

Supply trades start enabled. Purchases go into the player's inventory; deposit
them into a Quartermaster station or Village Armory for guards to use. Existing
Quartermasters receive offers for their current and earlier levels. Enable
optional gun sales under **Quartermaster Trades** before generating offers for
the relevant level. Saved offers keep their prices and uses; changing trade
settings or datapack definitions does not reroll previously generated levels.

Existing configurations retain their spawn-source choices. To allow automatic
gun loadouts from eggs, add **Spawn Egg** under **Eligible Spawn Sources**.
Starting-weapon rules, difficulty chances, and nearby gunner limits still apply.
Existing guards are not re-equipped.

The new **Peaceful Difficulty** section starts enabled for otherwise qualifying
new guards. Set its chance or nearby cap to zero to disable it. This does not
make Village Defense Buildings spawn guards on Peaceful.

## Compatibility

TaCZ **1.1.8 and newer for Minecraft 1.20.1** can load, including
**1.1.8-hotfix2**. Future TaCZ API changes can still require an add-on update.
Custom gun packs and mods that replace villager trading screens need their own
compatibility checks.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.20.1**, Java **17**, and Forge **47.4.10 or newer 47.x**.
- Guard Villagers **1.20.1-1.6.19**.
- Timeless and Classics Zero (TaCZ) **1.1.8 or newer for Minecraft 1.20.1**.
- Fzzy Config **0.7.6 for Minecraft 1.20.1**.
- Kotlin for Forge **4.12.0**.

Install the mod and all four required dependencies on both client and server.
