# Regional Village Armory drawings

The compact storehouse and supply yard now have dimensioned architectural
drawings for Desert, Savanna, Taiga, Snowy, deep ocean, Jungle, and Swamp.
They develop the [regional design specifications](regional-armory-designs.md).
These drawings and coordinate schedules now have corresponding
[furnished Minecraft previews](regional-armory-preview.md). The existing
[Plains previews](armory-preview.md) remain available separately.

Open the [drawing gallery](designs/armory/index.html) in a browser, or use the
individual SVG sheets below. Each sheet includes a floor plan, north and east
elevations, a store and roof section, and an entrance section. SVG drawings retain
their detail when zoomed. The [coordinate reference](designs/armory/coordinates.md)
lists doors, furniture, glazing, shutters, beams, lamps, roof footprints,
Armory anchors, and sentry positions in a readable form.

## Reading the sheets

The floor plan uses X east and Z south, with the road at Z=0. One square is
one block. Wall and window symbols reserve their floor footprints even when
the opening is higher on the wall. The Armory marked A is occupied furniture;
S is a separate service-sentry position. Small diamonds mark overhead lamps.
The roof is omitted from this view so the rooms and circulation remain clear.

Green circulation reservations connect the entrance, work areas, and service
area. A darker green 3 × 3 square marks service space immediately north of
the Armory. Blue marks a lobby or refuge, and pale ochre marks working space.
The white square in each yard is a 4 × 4 court kept open to the sky. A work
area can overlap a crossing where movement and working use share open floor;
furniture occupies separate cells. These are space reservations, not AI zones.

Paired doors have outside hinges. Solid side strokes show the intended open
leaves, while dashed strokes show their closed positions. Each leaf occupies
the normal lower and upper block positions. Door clearance remains an
exception to the three-air-block route target; these drawings do not establish
simultaneous guard passage or casualty-carrier clearance.

The north elevation shows the arrival facade. The east elevation looks west,
so north and the entrance appear at the right. Height rulers use local Y.
The colors indicate regional materials and the positions of glazing, timber,
roof, and lights. They are not Minecraft textures or lighting simulations.
The side view can show a canopy in front of another roof because it projects
all visible surfaces onto one plane.

The store section names its actual X or Z cut and includes the intersecting
walls, roofs, furniture, and fittings. Furniture follows the scheduled block
and slab heights, including the gap beneath each shelf's upper slab. A marks
the occupied Armory placeholder. The green three-block clearance band begins
at the standing surface and appears only over reserved routes; it does not
claim that furniture cells are empty. Roof material has enough thickness to
back the profile's risers;
the coordinate data retain separate roofs at different heights where their
footprints overlap. Native stair corners, precise seam finishing, and
attachment survival still require the later template preview.

The entrance section records the approach floor and door positions. Snowy's
section is unfolded along the route through its offset pairs; it is not one
straight cut through both doors. The floor plan shows the lateral turn.
The dashed line is the three-block headroom target, not a canopy surface.

## Snowy lobbies

The compact lobby reserves X=5..9, Z=4..6: five cells wide and three deep.
The outer pair occupies X=5..6 at Z=3, and the inner pair X=7..8 at Z=7.
The main room begins at Z=8, leaving the full turn between doors free of
shelves or benches. Its Armory is at (11,1,14), with the service area in
X=10..12, Z=11..13.

The yard lobby reserves X=6..10, Z=8..10. Its outer pair occupies X=6..7
at Z=7 and inner pair X=8..9 at Z=11. The workshop crossing occupies
X=2..7, Z=5..6, west of the open court at X=8..11, Z=2..5. The work bay
fits behind the crossing at X=2..4, Z=7..9. Both rows of the crossing are
covered. The sentry shelter starts east of the court at X=12, with its
support posts on the front row; it does not cover the open square.

This layout keeps the workshop, lobby, and supply room connected beneath
cover without filling the arrival court. The main room keeps its own
two-row cross-aisle and eastern supply bypass after the inner doors.

## Ocean landings and refuge space

Both ocean layouts place full deck blocks at Y=1, with feet at Y=2 and a
finite timber underside at Y=0. The architecture's first two-cell landing
has its top at Y=1.5 before the full Y=2 deck. The drawing labels the actual
provider-road height as unmeasured. A future jigsaw connector and its apron
must be aligned to the observed road; the design section alone cannot prove
a Tidal Towns join.

The compact store reserves X=10..12, Z=2..4 as a 3 × 3 refuge beside the
entrance. Its sentry stands at (3,2,2), on the other side. The deck crossing
at X=3..12, Z=4..5 connects both sides to the paired doors at X=6..7, Z=6.
The exterior roof posts stand outside the refuge and crossing.

The yard's eastern return passage occupies X=14..15, Z=3..12 and reaches
the rear refuge at X=14..16, Z=13..15. This is a turning bay at the end of
the passage: guards return toward the arrival deck along the same passage.
The drawing adds neither an exit over water nor a loop behind the rear wall.
Its internal room retains a separate two-cell passage at X=11..12 beside
the service area, so the exterior deck does not substitute for room access.

East-facing shutters are omitted beside this yard passage because their
reserved cells would overlap walking or refuge space. The windows retain
solid timber jambs. The remaining shutters sit beside glazing, with solid
wall backing at the same height; their placement does not rely on glass as
the supporting wall. Rail corners, half-step transitions, and neighboring
road blocks remain part of the later native placement checks.

| Taiga | Steep spruce gable extending over the compact store's deep porch and bench; a west work shed with its north end closed and a decorative crate beside its bench |
| Snowy | Furnishing-free offset lobbies; separate workshop crossing and sentry canopy; additional light positions for the enclosed approach |
| Deep ocean | Continuous contained deck, paired arrival lane, refuge spaces, a low sloping yard frontage roof, and an extra light over the yard's rear turning bay |
| Jungle | Taller clipped roof, shaded porch bench, and an additional upper glazed opening in the rear wall, framed in jungle timber |
| Swamp | Lower dark-oak roof, mud-brick infill, a porch bench, and a raised plank working deck; workshop slope stays below the main ridge |

The storehouse and yard retain one Village Armory each, with one crafting
table and decorative slab worktops, seats, wooden crate shapes, and shelves.
Shelves reserve two vertical cells; the proposed construction uses a solid
wooden base and upper slab. The designs add no inventories, profession blocks,
medical services, or new garrisons.

## Sources and checks

The [drawing author](../tools/draw_regional_armories.py) holds the proposed
geometry. It generates the sheets, coordinate reference, and
[machine-readable schedule](designs/armory/drawings.json) together. The schedule
is design data; no Minecraft build or world-generation loader consumes it.

```sh
python3 tools/draw_regional_armories.py
python3 tools/draw_regional_armories.py --check
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools/tests -p 'test_regional_armory_drawings.py'
```

The first command redraws the artifacts. The second checks the reservations
and verifies that the saved artifacts match the authoring source without
rewriting them. Checks cover supported floor reservations, furniture and
post conflicts, service alignment, open courts, covered work areas, light
support positions, shutter backing, bounds, and geometric route reachability
in four rotations. They also check that the side bypass remains reachable
when the service area is reserved as occupied.

The focused regression tests check slab heights and shelf gaps, occupied
Armories in the named sections, clearance markings, and the matching roof
profiles where the Taiga porch and ocean yard frontage join their stores.

These checks use proposed geometry with door cells treated as passable. They
do not simulate native door leaves, pathfinding, weather, lighting, combat,
or stock behavior. Exact native block states and lower-roof junction finishing
belong in the furnished preview pass, followed by actual packaged jars in an
ordinary Forge installation. Normal resupply must be observed separately from
scripted walking, because its current distance check can permit transfer
beside an exterior wall.

The [authoring and integration limits](regional-armory-designs.md#drawing-and-integration-boundaries)
still apply. In particular, the Snowy yard exceeds the current volume limit
once a one-row integration apron is added. The
[optional integration packs](regional-armory-integration.md) use a direct NBT
writer and a 96-definition runtime cap to accommodate the full combined family.
The drawings themselves do not modify any village pool.
