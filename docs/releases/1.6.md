# Villager Guard Gunners 1.6

Version 1.6 adds medics, casualty recovery, Village Quartermasters, and optional
village staffing and patrols. Guards can receive supplies, help injured allies,
and return to their duties after treatment.

## Highlights

- **Medic guards.** Stock a guard’s medical pouch with bandages or healing
  supplies and enable Medic Duty. Medics treat nearby allied guards using
  physical supplies. Medical Retreat lets injured guards seek help.
- **Casualty recovery and Recovery Cots.** Optional downed guards need one dose
  to stabilize and another to revive. With cot care enabled, medics can carry
  casualties to a free Recovery Cot. Default cot treatment takes 2 seconds,
  followed by 5 seconds of rest; emergency field treatment takes 8 seconds,
  followed by 20 seconds of rest. Travel and interruptions can add time.
- **Village Quartermasters.** Place and stock a Quartermaster station, then let
  an adult unemployed villager claim it. Enrolled guards can restock their
  current supplies or maintain a chosen equipment loadout during work hours.
  Existing equipment stays protected unless replacement is enabled. Linked
  Village Armories provide additional ammo.
- **Village-sized staffing.** Optional initial guard generation uses village
  size to set a shared allowance for ordinary guards and building garrisons.
  It affects newly generated villages and does not replace guards after deaths.
  Automatic gun loadouts have their own limits.
- **Automatic village patrols and readiness.** Uncommanded residents can rotate
  through village areas, including ordinary villages without Village Defense
  Buildings. Bells, threats, and raids change readiness. Saved squad routes and
  fixed building posts have separate controls.
- **Clearer settings.** Labels and tooltips explain feature permissions, units,
  patrol choices, and Quartermaster controls. Medical settings preview effective
  cot and field times. Related guard limits and Armory ammo thresholds reject
  incompatible edits, and building layout settings show restart notices.

## Fixes

- Casualties arrive on their cot’s mattress, remain aligned while resting, and
  get up beside it. Resting guards no longer swing their legs as if walking.
- Medics can retry another cot after a blocked route and still leave time for
  emergency field care. Broken cots preserve completed revival and elapsed rest.
- Improved patient selection in crowded villages and respect for follow orders.
- Fixed Armory inventory saving and slot order, canceled ammo-box drops, and
  repeated building activation attempts.
- Guards release unavailable patrol routes and skip unreachable waypoints.
  Invalid defense positions leave rotation until repaired.

## Updating and setup

Back up important worlds and replace the mod on both client and server. Keep
existing config files to preserve your choices. Restart Minecraft after updating.

Downed guards, cot care and transport, size-based initial staffing, and automatic
village patrols remain **disabled by default**. Enable the features you want in
Fzzy Config. Cot care requires Medical Treatment and Downed Guards; carrying
also requires a supplied guard with Medic Duty enabled and a reachable cot.
Individual guards start with Medic Duty and Medical Retreat off.

Quartermaster service is opt-in per guard. Choose **Restock** to replenish ammo
and medical item types already present, or **Loadout** to choose an equipment
profile. Seed an empty medical pouch yourself or use Loadout service. The
station’s **Pipes** switch controls hopper and pipe access separately.

Village Defense Building layout changes require reopening the world or
restarting the server and affect newly generated villages. Existing buildings
and guards keep their saved state.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.20.1** and Java **17**.
- Forge **47.4.10 or newer 47.x**.
- Guard Villagers **1.20.1-1.6.19**.
- Timeless and Classics Zero (TaCZ) **1.1.8-hotfix**.
- Fzzy Config **0.7.6 for Minecraft 1.20.1**.
- Kotlin for Forge **4.12.0**.

Install the mod and all four required dependencies on both client and server.
The baseline gun pack is the TaCZ Default Gun Pack bundled with TaCZ
1.1.8-hotfix. Additional gun packs need their own compatibility checks.
